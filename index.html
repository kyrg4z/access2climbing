About the Project

Creating safe spaces to grow

Access to Climbing creates free, monthly bouldering sessions for high school students aged 13–17 in Waterloo Region who face financial, social, or cultural barriers to sport.

Sessions are beginner-level, fully funded through community grants and donations, and supervised by certified gym staff and an adult advisor.

Hosted at Grand River Rocks — Waterloo and partnered with @grr_wat

Our Story

A personal journey to inclusion

Access to Climbing was founded by Artur Tashbolotov, a Grade 11 student in Waterloo Region who found a home in rock climbing after traditional sports proved inaccessible. Unlike other environments, climbing was welcoming, accessible, and deeply rewarding.

When Artur invited friends who also struggled to find their place in traditional athletics, the transformation was undeniable. They didn't just learn climbing skills—they built physical capability, confidence, and strong friendships, curing their feelings of loneliness.

Access to Climbing exists to share this spark. Our goal is to extend this experience to youth who feel they don't fit into traditional spaces, offering them a clear path to self-belief and community.

For Participants

Ready to climb?

Everything you need to sign up, prepare, and join our free monthly bouldering sessions at Grand River Rocks — Waterloo.

01

Sign Up

Open to all eligible youth ages 13–17 in Waterloo Region. Fill out the Google Form below to request a spot in an upcoming session.

Program Goal: To provide youth a chance to try climbing for free. We cannot provide consistent, recurring monthly sessions to the same individuals, allowing us to offer this initial experience to as many youth as possible.

Sign Up via Google Form
02

Get Approved

After signing up, you will be notified via email or SMS within 3 days regarding your approval.

Spots are limited. If the nearest session is full, you can opt to be booked for a session the following month!

03

Complete the Waiver

All participants must complete and submit a signed waiver before their first session. If the participant is under 18, a parent or legal guardian must sign.

  • Acknowledgment of physical risks involved in bouldering
  • Emergency contact information
  • Photo / media release consent (optional)
  • Agreement to follow all gym rules and staff instructions
Complete Waiver at Grand River Rocks
