About the Project

Creating safe spaces to grow

Access to Climbing creates free, monthly bouldering sessions for high school students aged 13–17 in Waterloo Region who face financial, social, or cultural barriers to sport.

Sessions are beginner-level, fully funded through community grants and donations, and supervised by certified gym staff and an adult advisor.

Partnered with @grr_wat, we're building a model for inclusive youth programming that can scale across the region.

Waiver & Safety

Safety is our top priority

All participants must complete and submit a signed waiver before their first session. If the participant is under 18, a parent or legal guardian must sign.

  • ✅ Acknowledgment of physical risks involved in bouldering
  • ✅ Emergency contact information
  • ✅ Photo / media release consent (optional)
  • ✅ Agreement to follow all gym rules and staff instructions
