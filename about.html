Our Story

Built by a climber, for everyone

We create free, one-time bouldering sessions for high school students aged 13–17 in Waterloo Region who face financial, social, or cultural barriers to sport. Every session is student-led, adult-supervised, and hosted at Grand River Rocks — Waterloo.

Our Story

A personal path to inclusion

Access to Climbing was founded by Artur Tashbolotov, a Grade 11 student in Waterloo Region who found a home in rock climbing after traditional sports proved inaccessible. Unlike other environments, climbing was welcoming, accessible, and deeply rewarding.

When Artur invited friends who also struggled to find their place in traditional athletics, the transformation was undeniable. They didn't just learn climbing skills — they built physical capability, confidence, and strong friendships, curing their feelings of loneliness.

Access to Climbing exists to share this spark. Our goal is to extend this experience to youth who feel they don't fit into traditional spaces — offering them a clear path to self-belief and community.
